# Quote & Reply

Select chat text and click **Quote reply** to write a response beside the selection. The preview shows up to three lines. Hover or click to see the full passage.

## Quotes and drafts

- To answer several passages together, click **+ Select another**, highlight the next passage, and click **Add quote**. Your reply and caret position are preserved.
- To write a separate reply, add the current reply to the draft first. Select the next passage and click **Quote reply** again.
- Reply chips stay in the order you added them at the top of the composer, above the draft text. Hover over a chip to read it in full or click `×` to remove that reply.
- Use the composer's send button to send everything together. Quotes are sent as Markdown blockquotes, and separate replies retain their paragraph breaks.

## Shortcuts

- Press `Enter` or click the blue up arrow to add the reply to the draft. The action is disabled for blank replies.
- Press `Shift+Enter` for a new line.
- Press `Esc`, click **Esc Cancel**, or click outside the popover to close it.
